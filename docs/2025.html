2025 city election

November 4, 2025. Four at-large council seats, no mayoral race. Last odd-year municipal election; winners serve three-year terms. Boulder Reporting Lab asked all eleven candidates the same six questions — every answer is below.

Lauren Folkerts, then mayor pro tem, finished sixth and left the council. Rob Kaplan, a former Boulder Rural Fire-Rescue captain, took the fourth seat. Totals: Boulder County Clarity ENR, last updated Nov 26 2025. Top four match the certified figures read at the Dec 4 2025 seating (Daily Camera).

City ballot measures

2A: Community, Culture, Resilience, and Safety (CCRS) tax extension

tax passed

Extends the existing 0.3% CCRS sales and use tax in perpetuity (it had been set to expire Dec 31 2036) without raising the rate. Funds capital work: rec centers, fire/police stations, paths, bridges, open space trailheads, etc.

Passed Nov 4 2025 (BRL Nov 6). Yes/no vote totals not copied from the SOV in this pass.

Boulder 2025 election results (BRL)

2B: CCRS tax debt authorization

bond passed

Authorizes up to $262 million principal ($350 million maximum repayment) payable solely from the CCRS tax extension, if 2A also passed. Companion to 2A.

Passed Nov 4 2025 (BRL Nov 6). Yes/no vote totals not copied from the SOV in this pass.
